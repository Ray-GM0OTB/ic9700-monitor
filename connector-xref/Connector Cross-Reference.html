Sources & limitationsBuilt from the Amphenol/Pyle MIL-DTL-83723 Series III catalog (Section B), the Conesys MIL-DTL-83723 Series III catalog, the Souriau-Sunbank / Proponent ESC10/EN2997 ordering information (SAE-ITC drawing ESC10), the Amphenol MIL-DTL-5015 Matrix crimp rear release catalog (pp. 433-460), and for ESC11 the Eaton Souriau 853 series catalog (8534 scoop-proof / ESC11, 8536 / ESC16) and the Amphenol Pyle catalog (pp. 20-26, 34-35, HTK series). BNK (and BJ, BJ8, BN, BN8) is from the Amphenol Pyle catalog How to Order page for Pyle commercial connectors designed to meet General Electric specifications (M50TF3564). D38999 / F38999 layouts, polarization angles (including Souriau T and V) and the hermetic options come from the Souriau 8D / D38999 Series III and MIL-DTL-38999 hermetic catalogs; F38999 is Souriau's non-QPL marking of the D38999 Series III interface. BACC63 styles and ordering codes come from the rms/Air Electro MIL-DTL-26500 catalog (BV, BP, CB, CC, BN), the Amphenol Boeing BACC63 38999 Series III how-to-order (CT, CU, DB, DC), the Amphenol BACC 5015 series pages (CD, CE), and Stealth Aerospace product pages (BR, CM); a BACC63 part is shown with its nearest military equivalent, which is not an approved substitute. M83723 Series I and II slash sheets and their MS replacements come from the Glenair military connector part number cross-reference; Series I dimensions from the MIL-DTL-26482 Series 2 MS3470 and MS3476 sheets. Illustration diameters come from the catalog dimension tables for each shell size; lengths are split from the catalog overall lengths. TE Deutsch 983 codes are read from TE product pages, which give the EN2997 or ESC15 number for each 983 part; the 983-15 accessory digit (4, 7) and class KV are not decoded. Pyle T-Line and its T3 contacts and sealing plugs come from the Amphenol Aerospace T-Line data sheet. TE Raychem TX / TXR backshell codes (interface code, shell code, entry size code) are read from TE and distributor datasheets for TXR40, TXR54, TXR21 and TXR18 parts; the finish letter C, option letters and variant suffixes are not decoded. Pyle BT, BY, BTY / BFY / BNY and HTK formats come from the Amphenol Pyle MIL-DTL-83723 Series III catalog (pp. 29, 33, 35, 36); Souriau 8534 and 8536 / ESC16 from the Eaton Souriau 853 series catalog. Cross-references are engineering guidance only; verify against the current specification sheets and with the manufacturer before ordering.
