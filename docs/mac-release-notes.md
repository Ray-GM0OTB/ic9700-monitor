IC9700 Monitor for the Mac (macOS 11 or later).

- **Apple silicon** (M1 and later – every Mac mini from late 2020 on): `…_mac_arm64.dmg`
- **Intel Macs:** `…_mac_x86_64.dmg`

Open the .dmg and drag **IC9700 Monitor** to **Applications**. The first time, macOS blocks it because it isn't from the App Store: open it once, then go to **System Settings > Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**.

See [docs/mac.md](https://github.com/Ray-GM0OTB/ic9700-monitor/blob/main/docs/mac.md) for the radio connection, permissions and differences from Windows. The `screenshot_mac_*.png` files show the app opening on GitHub's test Mac (no radio connected).
